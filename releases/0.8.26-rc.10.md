# Nightlink 0.8.26-rc.10 - Settings load recovery latency

Tracks [#109](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/109), [PR #110](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/110), and the pacing/progressive-display changes in [rc.9](0.8.26-rc.9.md).

The rc.9 physical check confirmed companion inspection within 1.6 seconds and progressive repeater values, then encountered a missing latitude reply. Direct core reads previously waited at least 30 seconds before recovering. Exact allowlisted read-only core queries now use a direct-route deadline of twice the companion estimate plus the existing margin, with an 8-second floor and capped by the existing overall reply budget. Flood/unknown routes, login, optional queries and configuration writes retain their prior deadlines. Writes never retry. The retry count, serialized duplicate settling and session guards remain unchanged.

Validation, provenance and final physical results follow below. No stable release is published.
