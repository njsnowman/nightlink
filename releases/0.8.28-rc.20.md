## Scope

Unread divider and Jump to latest were implemented/validated first (commit 890d1f1). Final scope also includes local reply/source navigation, contextual Reply/Copy/Path actions, consecutive sender grouping with individual time/hop details and conditional previous/next mention navigation. Reading positions persist per identity/conversation; background arrivals remain unread; anchors and action focus survive renders. Map iframe stays connected. See [behavior and limits](../docs/terminal-conversation-navigation.md).

Reply linkage is local history metadata, not a new radio thread protocol. The sender's normal text/mention remains the wire payload. Grouping and navigation affect main terminal Chat; existing Cards/DM/lock behavior remains supported.
