# Nightlink 0.8.22-rc.4 — local header controls candidate

Tracked in [#57](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/57) and [PR #52](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/52). See [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). Application source: e161bd4a826fa7a5b0ac369a69c802a896b9d596. Subsequent commits add tests/validation only.

## Changes

Top-right Search, Compact/Expand, connection and startup Cancel/Retry use original SVG icons from one family, matching 36px button height, 17px icons, border/radius, type, spacing, hover and keyboard focus. Radio status remains in the existing connection action; redundant live-radio badge and decorative iris are hidden. Dynamic connection text and compact labels retain their icons and original handlers. Accent palettes remain supported.

## Isolated checks

header-controls.browser.test.cjs passed three viewport widths, two text sizes, two layout modes, four accents and three connection states. Verified uniform metrics and alignment, no document overflow, startup long-label/Cancel fit, Search focus, Compact toggle and Connect dialog. Screenshot reviewed. startup-connect.browser.test.cjs passed startup success, limited retries, cancellation, sync-error pause and opt-in behavior.

Final app.asar with Electron 42.11.12 passed header-controls.native.test.cjs for dynamic labels/icons, uniform metrics, 130% text at 760x528, Search and Connect. startup-connect.native.test.cjs passed exact saved selection under synthetic discovery, synthetic synchronization to Overview and opt-out persistence. No radio APIs or production profile used.
