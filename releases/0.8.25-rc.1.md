# Nightlink 0.8.25-rc.1 validation record

Tracking: [#97](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/97). Local diagnostic candidate; not a public release.

## Change

Add the owner-approved Signal Rail to Overview/Messages channel and direct conversations and detached DM windows. LINK represents readiness of the local radio. RX briefly indicates a new incoming message. TX pulses during queue/send/retry. ACK pulses amber while waiting, turns green after a recipient acknowledgment, and remains amber for unconfirmed delivery. Channel acceptance lights TX green and does not imply recipient confirmation. Explicit send rejection lights FAULT red. Feedback expires to standby; reduced-motion settings suppress pulses. Retained history and sync backlog do not replay receive activity. Radio commands and retry policy are unchanged.
