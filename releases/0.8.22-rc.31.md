## Change

Add Contacts > Discover nodes with manual direct-range discovery, node-type filters, signal readings, tagged response correlation, bounded results and a cooldown. Require an explicit Add contact action; discovery never automatically saves identities. Add local saved-contact filtering by name, nickname, public key and type. Keep discovery and existing advert/share/import actions available under Radio tools without adding a main navigation item.

Discovery requires companion protocol 8 or later. Request format follows the firmware parser; discovery is zero-hop, not a mesh-wide inventory. Replies do not supply verified names or locations. See [protocol and limitations](../NODE_DISCOVERY.md).
