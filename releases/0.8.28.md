# Nightlink 0.8.28 stable release preparation

Tracks [#214](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/214) and the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). This is a preparation record. Publication requires owner approval of the verified artifacts and final notes.

## Scope

Nightlink 0.8.28 makes Terminal the default interface while keeping Cards available in Settings. It includes the offline Contact Book, Operations project and RF studies, and chat, reaction, CoreScope, and reconnect improvements from [PR #211](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/211). Duplicate RF reports were removed under [#209](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/209). On-screen studies, project records, and backups remain available.

## Validation plan

Build and verify the Windows x64 installer and portable executable from the stable-version commit. Verify the staged updater feed from 0.8.26. Run the Windows CI suite and clean install, upgrade, uninstall, and portable startup checks. Record the exact source revision and SHA-256 values alongside synthetic and physical evidence.

## Known validation limits

Private queued-message recovery remains unverified. [HQ USB identity diagnostics](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/116) remain open. The [rc.51 record](0.8.28-rc.51-post-merge.md) records tested radios, firmware, and the scope of the offline messaging evidence.
