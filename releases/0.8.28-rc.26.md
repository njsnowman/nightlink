# Nightlink 0.8.28-rc.26 local candidate

Tracks [startup restoration #159](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/159), [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and [design standard](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Based on rc.25; main app only. Queen City Con unchanged.

## Confirmed cause and change

A copied local-storage profile from the installed rc.25 app retained Cards mode, with no explicit startup-screen or startup-sound disable. The startup module returned early in Cards, and its overlay CSS was Terminal-only. The logo and approved WAV were still packaged and unchanged.

The approved ANSI startup overlay and its settings now work in both Cards and Terminal. Overlay-only VGA tokens/styles do not change the selected page interface. Preserve once-per-session behavior, explicit screen/sound disable, quiet hours, reduced motion, autoplay recovery, error/stall fallback and the media-ended dismissal gate. Active sound cannot be skipped by a pointer/key press. The intro does not configure or connect a radio; its separate status line observes actual connection state.
