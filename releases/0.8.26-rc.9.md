# Nightlink 0.8.26-rc.9 - Settings load latency

Tracks [#109](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/109) and [PR #110](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/110).

rc.8 imposed a one-second minimum timestamp wait on every remote command, even after a mesh reply had allowed the companion RTC to advance. rc.9 retains the initial session margin and waits only when the next unique timestamp is ahead of the reported companion clock. Commands remain serialized, strictly increasing and based on the companion RTC. Reply budgets and bounded read-only recovery are unchanged.

Device Center displays received values during inspection. Partial values stay disabled, and no editable/exportable snapshot exists until inspection completes. This improves first-result visibility without caching stale settings or parallelizing uncorrelated firmware replies.

Validation and physical timing follow below. This is a local candidate; physical configuration writes and direct infrastructure USB remain unverified.

Source validation: 286 unit tests pass. Device Center browser coverage verifies partial settings are visible and disabled while a later reply is held, with review/export blocked. Both source browser suites pass with synthetic radios.
