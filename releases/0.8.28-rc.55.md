## Scope

- Terminal chat readability (#213): received messages show the hop count and SNR on the nick line, colour-coded by distance and signal; more space between messages, an 88-character line limit and a thin left rule.
- Startup sound (#223): modem dial-up only (`startup-modem.wav`, 2.4 s); the rising tone and laser shot are removed. The owner approved the 2.4 s file by listening to it directly.
- Remove a channel from the radio (#224): Terminal `×` in the chat window list and `/part`; Cards list in the Add channel dialog; confirmation; slot cleared and read back; Public (slot 0) refused; saved history kept.
