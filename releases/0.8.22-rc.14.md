## Changes

Use zoom9 for initial radio-centered and Recenter views across CoreScope Overview/Network/echo, local Radio map and MeshMapper. MeshMapper without coordinates retains the region center with an explicit zoom9. Manual zoom/pan survives contact updates. Existing saved local viewport fallback and message-path route fitting are retained.

## Validation

- corescope-location.test.cjs: two passed. Coordinate validation, fallback, identity signature and consistent map context defaults.
- corescope-fallback.browser.test.cjs, echo-workspace.test.cjs, meshmapper.browser.test.cjs and meshmapper-region.browser.test.cjs passed. Radio-centered defaults, Recenter, manual pan preservation, region persistence, frame lifecycle/isolation, compact layout and offline/service fallback.
- corescope-fallback.native.test.cjs, meshmapper.native.test.cjs and meshmapper-region.native.test.cjs passed against built app.asar under matching Electron42.11.12, using isolated profiles and simulated service responses.
- Windows x64 NSIS and portable builds completed.

## Limitations

No physical radio, production profile or installed application changed. Zoom9 approximates the requested screenshot; geographic extent varies with panel size. Radio coordinates can be configured or stale. Remote sites and tile availability are independent of Nightlink. Prior installer visual-validation limitation and physical backlog acceptance in #49 remain open. Local candidate only; no stable release/tag published.
