# Nightlink 0.8.27-rc.10 diagnostic candidate

Pull request: [#120](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/120). Previous candidate: [rc.9](0.8.27-rc.9.md). [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

## Scope

rc.9 plus RF Propagation Monitor state persistence on quit and disconnect (commit `f6f441f`, applied on top of rc.9):

- `persist()` resolves when the IndexedDB write transaction completes.
- `NightlinkPropagationStore.flush(timeoutMs)` saves immediately. The tray quit handshake (`desktop:quit-request` in `tray-ui.js`) awaits `flush(2000)` before `readyToQuit()`; the main process keeps its 3 s cap.
- A radio disconnect saves immediately instead of waiting for the debounce.

Reason: the [rc.4 hardware record](0.8.27-rc.4.md) shows the monitor at 0 baseline hours after a clean quit and relaunch.
