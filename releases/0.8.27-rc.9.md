## Scope

BBS/ANSI graphics requested by the owner on 2026-11-02:

- ANSI art logo in `art/ansi/`: CP437 `.ans` with SAUCE record, 7-bit ASCII, mIRC-colored text and a rendered PNG. Night palette: letters fade from white to dark blue; antenna and mesh nodes in cyan. `make-art.py` (Python standard library) writes the art files and the in-app data module `ansi-logo-data.js`; `render-ansi.cjs` renders the PNG.
- The logo is shown in the connection dialog, at the top of Help > About and as a two-line sidebar wordmark. It is built as text with `textContent` and classes only, on its own dark card in every color scheme.
- Composer text art picker in the main window and detached DM: 24 original plain-text items in four groups. Items larger than the remaining 133-byte budget are disabled. Inserting never sends.
- Optional Terminal color scheme: colors and monospace fonts only. Radii, spacing, icons, focus and status colors are unchanged. Applies to the main window, detached DM and startup lock.
- ANSI SGR and mIRC formatting codes in chat and DM message text render as styled text. Other control sequences are stripped. Markup in message text stays text.
- The main composer markup now states the enforced 133-byte limit (previously a stale 180).
- CI `draft-release` job: after the package job, same-repository pull-request runs create a draft pre-release with both executables, `SHA256SUMS.txt` and `release-manifest.json`. Drafts are not public; the updater rejects drafts and pre-releases.
