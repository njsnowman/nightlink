# Nightlink 0.8.12 release record

Nightlink 0.8.12 adds conversation drafts and improves reading continuity.

- Save drafts by radio and conversation, shared across main and DM composers. Restore drafts after switching or reopening, and include them in local backups.
- Retain drafts after failed or unconfirmed sends. Clear matching submitted text after a recipient-acknowledged DM or radio-accepted channel broadcast; preserve newer edits. Broadcast acceptance does not confirm recipient receipt.
- Preserve floating/native DM reading anchors during arrivals, with navigation to newest messages and automatic following when already at the bottom.
- Keep an active @mention picker open during background redraws in the same conversation; persist selected mentions in drafts.

Compatibility

Windows x64. Close Nightlink and back up local data in Settings before installing. Existing %APPDATA%/Meshline data is retained. Drafts are local, unencrypted text and never sent automatically after reopen/reconnect. Storage failure warnings mean persistence cannot be guaranteed. Contact storage remains version 2 from 0.8.8; earlier version-1 clients may not open upgraded cards. Private-repository update discovery remains unavailable anonymously; use GitHub releases while signed in.

Validation

84 Node tests, draft browser checks and the fake-GATT messaging integration passed. Isolated packaged Electron checks verified native draft IPC, shared composers, reopen restoration and reading anchors. A packaged 0.8.11-to-0.8.12 payload replacement retained saved messages, contacts and contact notes; two conversation drafts survived a full app restart. This was an isolated application/profile upgrade test, not an installer-wizard upgrade over a real installation. No physical-radio test was performed.
