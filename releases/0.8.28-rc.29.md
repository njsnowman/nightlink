# Nightlink 0.8.28-rc.29 local candidate

Tracks [RF project planner #165](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/165), [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md), [project workflow and model limits](../docs/project-planner.md) and [design standard](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Based on rc.28. Main app only; Queen City Con unchanged. Startup-first-paint correction retained.

Operations now defaults to a seven-stage project workflow: brief/area, plotting and site roles, radio assumptions, batch terrain/LOS, common-area coverage, repeater gaps/candidate assessment and deployment. Existing transmitter selection and service targets are explicit. Gap proposals derive from evaluated below-budget cells; unknown terrain does not become a gap. Candidate evaluation separately records additional modeled area/target coverage and reciprocal backhaul. It does not identify verified available towers or guarantee RF service. Printable reports include the modeled grid and site packets; JSON/GeoJSON/CSV exports retain relevant inputs or derived data.

Coverage remains the documented sampled Bullington component plus free-space model, not ITM/Longley–Rice. The common study grid resamples existing radial predictions; source and sampling resolution, fetch dates and interpolation limitations are visible. Area/radio/site changes invalidate affected models. Network calculations remain explicit, bounded, cancellable and revision-protected. Completed terrain batches are cached; incomplete models are not saved.

Database version 4 retains prior operations and prevents older builds from writing it. Backup format 4 accepts imports 1–4. Legacy team/records/individual RF functions remain under Team & advanced tools. Common RF defaults derive from existing inputs where present; no transmitter role or study area is silently inferred.

Validation and artifact provenance follow before candidate download. Unsigned local candidate; no public release/production merge. No physical-radio or installed-upgrade evidence is claimed.
