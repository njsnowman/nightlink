## Features

Contact options > Compare public key displays the complete fingerprint and identity QR, reads a QR image or pasted key, and requires an explicit independent-comparison confirmation. Familiar names with different keys warn from saved comparison aliases. These local records do not authenticate channel sender names and are intentionally excluded from backup transfer.

Diagnostics > RF Trends summarizes existing passive packet observations by hour/day/week with count, SNR median/range, gaps and a conditional half-window comparison. It adds no radio probes. These are aggregate last-link observations across potentially different transmitters and bounded retention; they do not measure loss, coverage or per-hop quality.

Network > Radio map > Local points stores private locations, coverage notes and measurements with coordinates/notes. Edit/show/delete is local, with confirmation and safe text rendering. Points and accent preferences are backed up; restores preserve existing point IDs and report capacity skips. CoreScope cannot display these local overlays.

Signal Console refinement standardizes segmented corners, neutral message surfaces, typography, focus and compact dialogs across all four accents. Ice blue remains the default. Queue-notification catch-up and stale generation completion fixes are included.
