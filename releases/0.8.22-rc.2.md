# Nightlink 0.8.22-rc.2 — local navigation candidate

Tracked in [#55](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/55) and [PR #52](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/52). See [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). Application source: 12ed2a612e78a71cc3e14ddf3eaee83672b308b2. Subsequent test/documentation commits do not change the packaged application.

## Scope

Five primary destinations: Overview, Messages, Network, Contacts and Radio. Messages contains Channels and Direct messages. Search remains in the top bar with Ctrl+K. Tools contains Activity and Diagnostics. Optional sidebar conversation shortcuts start collapsed; recent contacts no longer repeat the directory. The Messages badge combines channel and direct-message unread counts. Incoming updates retain the channel selector DOM/focus and manual Tools expansion. Compact controls honor text scaling. Preview server serves the new navigation assets.

## Validation

- navigation.browser.test.cjs: passed, 350 synthetic contacts and 20 channels, four viewport sizes and compact states, searchable directory/contact card, composer/Settings reachable and no outer scrolling.
- navigation-flows.browser.test.cjs: passed, combined unread/read transitions, silent recovered messages, channel switching, searchable DM inbox, one popup, retained channel selection, picker focus and manual Tools state.
- attention.browser.test.cjs: passed, unread persistence, latest-message following, mute persistence, independent sound settings, mention navigation and focused DM reading. Migrated test navigation to current controls and self-served source.
- Independent compact review: 760x560, 130% text, both compact states and nine destinations; controls fit with no document overflow.
- navigation.native.test.cjs against final app.asar with Electron 42.11.12: passed at content sizes 760x528, 760x600, 920x700 and 1320x860 in both compact states; composer, Settings, panels and searchable contact card accessible.
- accent-theme.native.test.cjs against final app.asar: passed, four accents propagate to native DM, draft/pref persistence, direct resolved recipient, child contact card routing and single native DM.
- echo-workspace.test.cjs: passed, echo-only split, iframe lifecycle, divider, 130% compact fit and offline/local restoration.

All checks used isolated synthetic browser/native profiles. No RF sends, physical radio tests or production profile changes. These results do not resolve the outstanding physical nonempty-backlog and two-radio acceptance in #49.
