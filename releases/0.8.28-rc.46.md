# Nightlink 0.8.28-rc.46 local candidate

Tracks [local planning tools #199](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/199), [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and [design standard](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Based on rc.45. Queen City Con unchanged.

Saved ideas/projects can open Local planning tools. This optional workspace works from an independent source snapshot. Six tools share a map and right panel: point budget inspection; bounded boundary/exclusion terrain screening; sampled terrain/obstacle/3D inspection; GPX/GeoJSON route classification; parametric antenna/profile experiments and local presets; direct endpoint measurement residuals. No CloudRF SDK, API, account or dependency is added.

Local previews use stored terrain. Terrain-aware Bullington and a clearly labeled free-space baseline use existing calculations. Parametric HPBW/aim/downtilt patterns are idealized entered approximations, not manufacturer patterns. Ground-relative obstacles require declared bare-ground inputs; existing surface data is not silently treated as bare ground. Obstacles are intersected at existing samples, so narrow features may be missed. 3D depicts those same sampled paths; unsampled surroundings are not inferred. CSV terrain requires source/date and datum; coordinates use WGS84. Display distances are miles and heights are feet.

Area screening is a bounded coarse grid of up to 25 allowed positions and up to eight shortlisted positions. It uses the original saved baseline, not local antenna/obstacle variants. Cached-only is selected initially; online terrain lookup is explicit and cancellable. Full coverage/backhaul and site-access evaluation remain required. Keep candidate is a separate action with current source/revision checks; it copies only a proposed location. It does not promote an installed site or measured coverage.

Routes preserve separate GPX track segments, classify edges from sampled endpoints and retain unknown lengths. They do not simulate packet routing or verify continuous service. Measurement CSV imports require direct/setup confirmation and current matching inputs/location/frequency/receiving height; stale/mismatched rows are excluded. Statistics are residuals against predictions, not proof of area coverage or probabilistic confidence. Source observations are retained separately.

Studies and presets use a separate local IndexedDB database (nightlink-local-studies v1). Source project/database/backups remain version 8 and unchanged. Save local study creates a new local version; Export study bundle includes its normalized source snapshot, inputs and imported observations. Studies/presets are not included in normal project backups. Screening previews are transient and are rerun rather than silently reused after reopening. Presets contain equipment/environment assumptions, not coordinates; they require review before application.
