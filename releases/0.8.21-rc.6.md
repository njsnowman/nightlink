# Nightlink 0.8.21-rc.6 — local accent palette candidate

Tracked in [#51](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/51), [PR #52](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/52). See [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). Application source: 4bcce1f5fb2ae12f0b0df32d48c09f7ae1d2302e.

## Behavior

Ice blue (#8CBFFF) is the default display accent. Settings > Display accent offers ice blue, amber (#FFB547), emerald (#42D6A4), and violet (#A78BFA). The local preference persists across reloads and propagates to open native DM windows. The former accent selector is removed. Panel corner highlights, selected navigation, primary actions and hop badges use the selected palette; delivery success, warning and error colors remain semantic. Message text remains neutral. The presentation does not change radio settings.

## Validation

accent-theme.native.test.cjs passed against final app.asar with Electron 42.11.12, isolated profile and simulated identity: ice-blue default, no duplicate old selector, all four palettes propagated to the DM, unsent draft preserved, saved violet preference restored after reload, contact-card ownership restoration and one native DM after repeated contact actions.

signal-console.browser.test.cjs passed keyboard navigation at 760×560 with 130% text, message hop ordering, long DM wrapping and no outer DM scrolling. Settings was visually inspected in a browser preview. No physical-radio or production-profile testing was performed.
