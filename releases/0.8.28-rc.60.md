# NightLink 0.8.28-rc.60 candidate

Design pass, batch 2 (owner request, 2026-11-27: items 4, 7 and 8). [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md#unreleased). Previous candidate: [rc.59](0.8.28-rc.59.md). Unpublished candidate for owner evaluation.

## Scope added since rc.59

- Nick colours (`terminal.js`, `terminal.css`): Settings > Nick colours, either Single colour (default; the owner-approved cyan treatment stays the default) or By sender. By sender uses the existing name-hash classes `mrc-nc-0` to `mrc-nc-7` with VGA colours of at least 4.5:1 on black; yellow is left for the user's own nick. The setting is stored in `nightlink.term-nicks` and applied as `data-term-nicks` on every document.
- Sound packs (`terminal-sound.js`): Modem (sine FSK warble) and BBS bells (triangle chimes) join Standard and Retro. They cover the same events (sent, DM, mention, connect chirp) and each sound is at most 0.6 s.
- Status line: `DM n` (magenta button to Direct Messages) when direct messages are unread; `NF x dBm` from the radio stats noise field. Also refreshes on `stats` events.

## Validation (simulated)

- `npm test`: unit suite passes, including the sound-pack test extended to every pack and duration.
- `terminal-nicks.browser.test.cjs` (new): single cyan by default; By sender gives at least four distinct colours over ten senders, each at least 4.5:1, and persists after reload.
- `terminal-statusline.browser.test.cjs`: extended for DM count show/hide and the noise floor.
