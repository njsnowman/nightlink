## Changes

Add compact lazy webpage cards to channel/Overview messages and direct conversations, with site-provided thumbnail/title/description/domain and original text retained. Existing image/Giphy previews remain separate. Settings → Webpage previews opts out and updates open DM snapshots. Page HTML/scripts are not embedded. Native metadata/image retrieval uses validated, pinned public IPv4 HTTPS destinations with redirect, size, time, queue and cache limits, no browser cookies/authentication/referrer. Native IPC is restricted to unlocked main/registered DM frames.

## Limitations

Cards use website metadata, not literal rendered-page screenshots. Authenticated, JavaScript-only, blocked, IPv6-only or proxy-only pages can fall back to a domain/link card; missing or oversized thumbnails are omitted. Previews require Internet and contact the linked sites. Already submitted operations may finish after opt-out. Metadata is not verified identity or endorsement. No physical radio, installed app, user profile or user credentials were accessed/changed.

Prior installer visual-validation limitation from rc.10 and physical backlog acceptance in #49 remain open. Startup app-lock security boundary remains as documented in SECURITY.md. Local candidate only; no stable release/tag published.
