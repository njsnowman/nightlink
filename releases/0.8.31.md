# Nightlink 0.8.31 stable release preparation

Tracks [#241](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/241) and the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). This is a preparation record. Publication requires the owner's approval of the verified artifacts and of the physical-radio checks below.

## Scope

Cards skins (owner request), merged in #240 (merge commit `6bc45e8`). Settings > Interface > Skin offers:

- Standard (default, with Glass)
- Tactical
- Red light
- Radio panel
- School / District

School / District takes a district name, shown at the top of the sidebar, and a district colour. A colour is refused when white text on it would be below 4.5:1. The skins apply in the main window, the detached DM window and the startup lock. Glass applies to Standard only. Terminal is unchanged.

Version: `package.json` 0.8.31. Installations of 0.8.30 are offered 0.8.31.

## Draft release notes

Title: `Nightlink 0.8.31`. Tag: `v0.8.31`.

```
**Cards skins**
Settings > Interface > Skin changes the look of the Cards interface:
- Standard: the default look, with Glass.
- Tactical: grey-green panels with corner brackets and readout-style text.
- Red light: everything red on black to protect night vision, including avatars, status lights and maps.
- Radio panel: brushed-metal panels and buttons, with an amber chat display.
- School / District: a light, plain look with your district's name and colour. Colours too light for white text are refused.

Skins also apply to direct-message windows and the startup lock. Glass applies to the Standard skin only. The Terminal interface is unchanged.

**Known limits**
The Windows executables are unsigned. #116 remains open. Channel send confidence is deferred to #230.

To upgrade, export a Nightlink backup, quit Nightlink from the tray icon, and run the Windows installer. Check the downloads against the attached SHA256SUMS.txt.
```
