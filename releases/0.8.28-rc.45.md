# Nightlink 0.8.28-rc.45 local candidate

Tracks [planning map selection #197](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/197), [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md) and [design standard](../DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). Based on rc.44. Queen City Con unchanged.

Operations Map & layers offers Streets, U.S. aerial imagery, topographic, imagery with contours, shaded relief and an explicit imported offline pack. A compact keyboard-accessible popover keeps the map in place and closes after style selection. Preferences persist locally. Source heatmap/path/site/candidate panes and heatmap opacity change display only; scenario/field-evidence layers retain their controls and remain above source overlays. Pins, viewport, saved models and RF elevation inputs do not change when selecting a map. Imagery retains natural colors in both themes. Scale stays miles; entered heights stay feet. Provider-printed tile labels retain their units.

USGS choices disclose U.S. coverage, variable imagery dates/detail and a conservative native zoom limit of 16, with enlargement through map zoom 19. Attribution remains attached to the actual source. Offline USGS does not silently display an unrelated pack; offline packs retain their own source credit. Existing Streets can use an imported pack while offline. Requests are limited to visible tiles, with failure/retry and bounded tile timeouts; no bulk download, paid account or API key is added. This increment does not add a new 3D renderer or replace the RF terrain dataset.
