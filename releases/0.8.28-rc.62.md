# NightLink 0.8.28-rc.62 candidate

Field-use batch (owner request, 2026-11-27: items 9, 1 and 5 of the second enhancement list). [Changelog](../CHANGELOG.md#unreleased). Previous candidate: [rc.61](0.8.28-rc.61.md). Unpublished candidate for owner evaluation.

## Scope added since rc.61

- Item 9, auto-reconnect visibility: `recovery.js` already reconnects with backoff (Settings switch `auto-reconnect`). The status line now listens to its `recovery` events and shows `retry N in Xs` (one-second countdown), `reconnecting`, or `retry stopped` while the link is down.
- Item 5, pinned messages (`pins.js`, `terminal-mrc.js`): `/pin`, `/pins` and `/unpin [n]`; at most 5 per conversation, stored in `nightlink.pins.<identity>`. The `[PIN]` bar is sticky inside the log and carries no message ids, so the reading position and append fast path are unaffected.
- Item 1, channel send confidence: **not implemented.** Raw RX log frames (0x88) are decoded in `contacts-ui.js` (`MeshlineRoutes.parseRaw` and `decodeRoute`) to attach observed routes to *received* channel messages. Whether the companion reports repeated copies of the user's *own* packets, rather than dropping them as duplicates, needs a physical-radio capture. Tracked in [#230](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/230).
- Fix, owner report 2026-11-27 (screenshot): LAST SNR showed `-` for every repeater, because rc.59 used only the SNR of direct messages, which repeaters do not send. `node-signal.js` now also takes the SNR the companion measured on the final hop of received messages' observed routes. That hop must resolve to exactly one repeater; ambiguous path hashes are skipped. A zero-hop packet counts for its sender. The tooltip says when it was heard.
