# Nightlink 0.8.28-rc.48 local candidate

Tracks [chat contact saves #203](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/203) and the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).

Click a sender in chat to open their contact card, then choose **Save to Contact Book**. Terminal nicknames accept keyboard activation. The card carries available radio identity, advert time, location, last available SNR, flags, saved outbound path, modification time and local nickname, notes and photo. Senders without a full key can be kept by their observed chat name and any available key prefix. The saved entry can be linked to a radio identity later. Repeated saves reuse the matching entry and retain its personal edits. A newer radio snapshot refreshes the saved identity while preserving the last known location when coordinates are unavailable.
