## Scope

Includes the rc.12 readability changes, plus aligned message bodies below sender headers, a chat-only Small/Medium/Large text-size control (14/16/18px, Medium by default), preference persistence and detached-window synchronization. Technical details retain their base size and delivery colors; hop summaries stay prominent. Unrelated MRC indexing changes are excluded.
