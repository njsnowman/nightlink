# Nightlink 0.8.5 release record

Tracking: https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/4
Status: preparation; not yet published.

## Scope

- Bluetooth command serialization waits for GATT write completion and the companion response. Discovery retries are bounded; synchronization errors identify the failed command and pause automatic recovery. (#1)
- Retained-message recovery is prioritized and deduplicated after reconnect. Background polling is sequential.
- Readable text sizes, improved contrast, and persistent text-size settings. (#2)
- Connection console with event-driven progress, cancellation and reduced-motion support. (#3)
- Direct Messages navigation, sender resolution and channel mention autocomplete included since 0.8.0.

## Physical validation

On 2026-10-15, the user reported more than four hours of continuous Bluetooth connection and confirmed that retained messages recovered after reconnect without duplication and in the correct channels. This applies to the tested radio and environment. Issue #1 is closed.

## Known limitations

DM retry/flood behavior remains simulation-tested; no additional physical two-radio verification is claimed. Recovery only includes messages received and retained by the powered radio. Finite queue limits and RF outages can leave unrecoverable gaps. Uncached street tiles require internet.
