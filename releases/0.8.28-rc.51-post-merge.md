## Physical-radio evidence and remaining checks

The [integration PR](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/pull/211) records offline USB sync, telemetry, remembered-port reconnect, live Public and private reception, and nonempty Public queue recovery on a Heltec V4.3 OLED running `v1.18.0-81dbae5`. These tests used the integration payload before merge.

On November 19, the owner confirmed receipt on HQ of outbound private-channel broadcasts from both stable 0.8.26 and the post-merge candidate, using the same offline USB companion. A subsequent synchronized test used the post-merge packaged renderer with both radios offline. SN0MAN 3 sent a direct message over COM19 to HQ on Bluetooth. HQ recorded the exact message, and SN0MAN 3 recorded a recipient acknowledgment in 645 ms.

Before the direct test, the owner authorized removing one non-favorite on the full HQ roster. The test protected favorites marked on the radio or saved in Nightlink and verified the selected removal by exact key. Both radios then saved and read back the other radio’s live identity as a companion contact. Earlier direct tests received no acknowledgment on stable or the candidate. The recorded pass applies to the synchronized setup with reciprocal contacts.

Private queued-message recovery remains unverified. The USB identity timeouts observed on the separate HQ setup remain open under [#116](https://github.com/njsnowman/nightlink/issues/116). Review the remaining evidence and release scope before publication.
