## Scope

Owner request on 2026-11-03: a better ANSI logo. Presentation only.

- terminal-logo.js `chrome()`: 12-pixel letterforms with 3-pixel strokes (N, I, G, H, T, L, K), each pixel a half cell (8 x 8), three-pixel letter spacing, a one-pixel lean per six rows, a solid chrome ramp (white and bright cyan to dark cyan above a sharp horizon; bright cyan, bright blue and blue below) and a dithered dark-grey drop shadow. Colors are VGA only. `logo()` now draws this; the earlier 5x7 `cells()` remains exported.
- The main menu draws the logo up to 960 px wide.
- `art/ansi/make-chrome.cjs` exports the logo as CP437 ANSI (`nightlink-chrome.ans`, classic SGR: bold for bright foregrounds, backgrounds 40-47, no iCE colors), UTF-8 ANSI and plain text; `nightlink-chrome.png` is the `render-ansi.cjs` preview. In the CP437 file, rows where both half-pixels are bright use the nearest normal background for the lower half.
